BEHIND THE HELMET

MEET CHRIS PARSONS

A face from a galaxy far, far away.

Chris Parsons was part of the original Star Wars trilogy, appearing in a remarkable range of roles across The Empire Strikes Back and Return of the Jedi.

Best known to fans as 4-LOM, his work also brought a number of droids, soldiers and background characters to the screen. This site celebrates those performances through photographs, film memories and personal memorabilia.
